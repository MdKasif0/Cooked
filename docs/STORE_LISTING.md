## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Ever set a reminder, swiped it away in two seconds, and completely forgot about it until it was too late? 

**You're Cooked.**

Cooked is the anti-procrastination deadline tracker and nagging reminder app built for college students, freelancers, engineers, and ADHD brains who need real urgency to get things done.

Most reminder apps ping once and politely disappear. Cooked doesn’t play nice. It pesters you repeatedly with escalating notifications and optional full-screen waking alerts until you actually tick the task as Done.

### 🔥 WHY COOKED WORKS: THE HEAT ENGINE
Deadlines don't sneak up; they heat up. Cooked automatically computes your remaining workload against your real waking hours and classifies urgency into 4 heat stages:
- **Chill (Green)**: Plenty of breathing room. Relax.
- **Warm (Yellow)**: Time to start chipping away.
- **Toasty (Orange)**: The pressure is mounting. Start now to stay sane.
- **Cooked (Deep Ember)**: Zero slack remaining. Work or burn.

### ⏱️ SMART "START-BY" GUIDANCE
Stop doing midnight mental math. Cooked calculates your exact **Start-By date and time** by subtracting your remaining effort and a 1-day safety buffer from the final due date.

### 🗣️ THREE NAGGING PERSONALITIES
Customize the voice that talks to you:
- **Mild**: Calm, polite, no-nonsense nudges.
- **Spicy (Default)**: Witty sarcasm that calls out your procrastination.
- **Unhinged**: Relentless, borderline unhinged roasting that forces you to cave.

### ⚡ NATURAL LANGUAGE QUICK ADD
Type or dictate naturally:
- *"Submit compiler assignment fri 5pm 2h !!"*
- *"Dentist appointment tomorrow 10am"*
- *"Renew car insurance in 3 days"*
Cooked understands relative times, durations, recurrence, and urgency marks instantly with zero internet required.

### 📲 GLANCE HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
Keep urgency visible without opening the app:
- **Quick Add**: One-tap floating creation sheet.
- **Next Up**: Next 3 items with direct checkbox actions.
- **Countdown**: Giant tabular countdown with live heat bar for your most critical deadline.
- **Cooked Score**: Live visual gauge measuring your weekly completion momentum.

### 📊 SHAME-FREE INSIGHTS
No artificial streaks that reset when life happens. Cooked gives you Spotify Wrapped-style productivity truths:
- **Nags Per Win**: *"You usually cave after 2.4 nags."*
- **Snooze Habits**: See which hours you snooze the most.
- **Lateness Median**: *"When late, you're only ~35m late. Respectable."*
- **On-Time Rate**: Track how your reliability improves over time.

### 🔒 100% OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVATE
- **No account required**: No signups, no passwords, no email collection.
- **Your data stays on your device**: All reminders, notes, and history live in a private SQLite database.
- **Zero data brokers**: We don't sell your data, track your location, or transmit your tasks to external servers.
